# The Precepts — Noi

Keep this where you'll see it. The feed recontaminates itself from daily habits; these keep it clean.

1. Do not click ads out of curiosity.
     — A click is the single strongest 'I want this' signal you can send.

2. Do not linger on an ad you don't want.
     — Time-on-screen (dwell) is measured. Scroll past deliberately.

3. Do not react, comment, or argue on sponsored posts.
     — Any engagement — even angry — reads as 'show me more of this'.

4. Do not follow or like brand pages you don't want to hear from.
     — A follow is a standing invitation the algorithm honors for months.

5. Mind your searches.
     — Product searches inside Meta feed the ad engine. Search elsewhere.

6. Guard the back door.
     — New browser, new phone, or accepting cookie tracking lets off-site pixels refill your profile. Re-clear off-Meta activity monthly.

7. Feed good seed.
     — Engage honestly with what you DO love. The feed grows toward your genuine attention — so aim it on purpose.

8. Renew the practice.
     — Once a month, re-clear off-Meta activity and hide any new junk advertisers. Ten minutes keeps the whole thing clean.
