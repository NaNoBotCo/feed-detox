# Feed Detox playbook — Noi
_Generated 2026-07-13_

Do these once, in order. About 15 minutes. You stay logged in the whole time — nobody but you touches your account.

**Where the controls live**
- Phone: tap the **menu (≡)** → **Settings & privacy** → **Settings** → scroll to **Ads** / **Ad preferences**.
- Computer: top-right menu → **Settings & privacy** → **Settings** → **Ads** in the left sidebar.

(Meta moves these around. If a name doesn't match, search the Settings page for the word in **bold**.)

## Step 1. Cut off the outside data supply
Chosen level: **Moderate** — Clear history now, but leave future activity connected. Good balance; re-clear monthly (see the Precepts).

Go to **Off-Meta activity** (older name: *Off-Facebook activity*).
1. Tap **Clear history** / **Disconnect** to wipe what's stored.
2. Leave future activity connected — but re-clear monthly (Precept 6 & 8).

## Step 2. Prune the interest categories
Open **Ad preferences → Categories used to reach you** (a.k.a. *Interest categories*). This is the profile Meta built about you. Remove anything that doesn't match what you want.

Hunt down and **remove** anything related to your see-less list:
- Alcohol
- Politics & social issues

**Keep** (or don't worry about) categories matching what you *do* want:
- Books & reading
- Cooking & food
- Travel

## Step 3. Turn down sensitive ad topics
Open **Ad topics**. Facebook lets you say **See less** on a set of sensitive topics directly. Set **See less** on any that apply:
- Alcohol → **See less**
- Politics & social issues → **See less**

## Step 4. Hide the junk advertisers
Open **Advertisers → Advertisers you've seen recently** (and **...whose ads you clicked**). For every one you don't want:
- Tap it → **Don't show ads from this advertiser** / **Hide**.
Be ruthless. This list is where most of the daily clutter comes from.

## Step 5. Plant good seed
The feed needs positive signal, not just deletions. Give it honest signal toward what you like:
- Follow 1–2 genuinely good pages about **Books & reading**.
- Follow 1–2 genuinely good pages about **Cooking & food**.
- Follow 1–2 genuinely good pages about **Travel**.
- When a *good* ad appears, it's fine to let it be. You're teaching the algorithm what 'right' looks like.

## Step 6. Learn the everyday tool
From now on, on ANY ad you don't want:
- Tap the **···** (three dots) on the ad → **Why am I seeing this ad?** → **Hide ad** or **Hide all ads from this advertiser**.
This is the 3-second move that keeps the feed clean forever.

---
## The Precepts — how not to recontaminate your feed

The cleanup above is one-time. Staying clean is a practice. The feed rebuilds itself from what you do every day — so live by these:

1. **Do not click ads out of curiosity.**
   _Why:_ A click is the single strongest 'I want this' signal you can send.

2. **Do not linger on an ad you don't want.**
   _Why:_ Time-on-screen (dwell) is measured. Scroll past deliberately.

3. **Do not react, comment, or argue on sponsored posts.**
   _Why:_ Any engagement — even angry — reads as 'show me more of this'.

4. **Do not follow or like brand pages you don't want to hear from.**
   _Why:_ A follow is a standing invitation the algorithm honors for months.

5. **Mind your searches.**
   _Why:_ Product searches inside Meta feed the ad engine. Search elsewhere.

6. **Guard the back door.**
   _Why:_ New browser, new phone, or accepting cookie tracking lets off-site pixels refill your profile. Re-clear off-Meta activity monthly.

7. **Feed good seed.**
   _Why:_ Engage honestly with what you DO love. The feed grows toward your genuine attention — so aim it on purpose.

8. **Renew the practice.**
   _Why:_ Once a month, re-clear off-Meta activity and hide any new junk advertisers. Ten minutes keeps the whole thing clean.
